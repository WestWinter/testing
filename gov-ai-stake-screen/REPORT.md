# The State as Shareholder: Front-Running the Next US Government Equity Stake in the AI Stack

**Prepared 2026-07-06 · Universe: 102 US-listed names · Method: reverse-engineered selection doctrine → 9-factor weighted screen → back-cast validated**

---

## 1. Executive summary

The Trump administration has built, in under a year, the largest peacetime portfolio of corporate equity positions in US history: Intel (9.9%), MP Materials (~15%), Lithium Americas (5% + 5% of the Thacker Pass JV), Trilogy Metals (10% + warrants), USA Rare Earth (LOI for 8–16%), nine quantum companies including IBM and GlobalFoundries ($2.0B for minority stakes), a proposed $1B convert into L3Harris's rocket-motor unit, revenue shares on Nvidia/AMD China chips (15%, reportedly 25% on the newest deal), a golden share in US Steel, and a 20% profit-share-plus-IPO-warrant structure over Westinghouse. In June, the president said he is considering stakes in "top AI companies," and OpenAI has proposed donating 5% of itself to seed a US "Public Wealth Fund" — with the reported concept extending to every leading US AI developer, including Alphabet and Meta.

**The single most important analytical finding:** the government does not "pick AI companies." It prices *leverage*. Every completed deal converts something the government already controls — an unpaid grant, a distressed loan, an export license, a permit, a merger approval — into equity or equity-like upside in a company that (a) controls a domestic chokepoint in the AI/defense supply chain and (b) needs something from Washington. That is why the "AI stakes" to date have landed *upstream* of the AI applications layer: chips, rare earths, uranium enrichment, quantum, rocket motors. The screen therefore rewards federal hooks, chokepoint status, and need — not AI purity.

## 2. Doctrine memo: how the administration actually picks

### 2.1 The paper trail

- **SWF EO (Feb 3, 2025)** — ["A Plan for Establishing a United States Sovereign Wealth Fund"](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/a-plan-for-establishing-a-united-states-sovereign-wealth-fund/) directed Treasury/Commerce to design a fund to "monetize the asset side of the U.S. balance sheet." A formal fund needs Congress and was quietly shelved ([Protect Democracy](https://protectdemocracy.org/work/trumps-sovereign-wealth-fund-plan-explained/)); the *function* was implemented anyway, deal by deal, with stakes parked at Commerce, DoD, and DOE. The [DFC is being repositioned as a SWF-like vehicle](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-24/us-lending-agency-wants-to-be-more-like-a-sovereign-wealth-fund) (Bloomberg, Feb 2026).
- **America First Investment Policy (Feb 2025)** — investment as security policy; adversary supply chains as the organizing threat.
- **AI Action Plan, "Winning the Race" (Jul 2025)** — compute, energy, and the full AI stack as a national race; datacenter permitting relief; export of the US stack.
- **EO "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security" (Jun 2, 2026)** — voluntary 30-day pre-release government access to frontier models; Treasury/NSA/CISA framework due **Aug 1, 2026**; classified cyber benchmarking; explicitly no licensing regime ([White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/), [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/06/new-ai-executive-order), [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/06/promoting-advanced-ai-innovation-and-security-executive-order-top-points)). This is the regulatory-relationship scaffolding onto which equity conversations are being grafted.
- **Jun 5, 2026** — Trump says he is [considering government stakes in top AI companies](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/06/05/tech-leaders-will-discuss-government-stakes-top-ai-firms-trump-says/) and directs national-security agencies to onboard "the most advanced AI models from multiple vendors" ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/trump-open-ai-altman-stake.html)).
- **Jul 2, 2026** — FT-reported: [OpenAI proposes handing the administration a 5% stake](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/02/business/openai-trump-stake-intl) (~$42.6B at an $852B valuation) to seed a "Public Wealth Fund," within a concept where **each leading US AI developer — Anthropic, Google, Meta named — cedes ~5%** via a government vehicle ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/openai-proposes-us-government-own-5percent-stake-to-address-political-blowback.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/02/openai-proposed-donating-5-of-its-equity-to-a-us-sovereign-wealth-fund/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/02/openai-stake-trump-altman)).
- **Officials, on the record:** Lutnick — the Intel stake was "converting a grant into equity for the American people"; the administration is ["thinking about" stakes in defense contractors: "Lockheed Martin makes 97% of their revenue from the US government"](https://thehill.com/business/5470771-trump-administration-defense-contractors/). Bessent — "Other countries, in essence, are providing us with a sovereign wealth fund." Lutnick/Burgum — stakes reduce dependence on Taiwan (chips) and China (minerals). A Trump official: [more stakes in minerals companies are coming](https://www.mining.com/web/us-plans-more-stakes-in-minerals-companies-trump-official-says/).

### 2.2 The revealed deal template

Across every completed transaction (full terms in §5 and the Excel "Deal Precedents" tab), the same five-part template recurs:

1. **Leverage first.** The government almost never writes a *new* check for equity. It converts money or permission already on the table: unpaid CHIPS grants (Intel), a distressed DOE loan (Lithium Americas), export licenses (Nvidia/AMD), a mining permit and road approval (Trilogy), merger clearance (US Steel), CHIPS R&D awards (quantum-9), an EXIM/loan package (USA Rare Earth).
2. **Chokepoint target.** The recipient is the sole or scarce domestic source of an input the AI/defense stack cannot function without: the only US rare-earth mine, the only US-owned leading-edge logic hope, the only US large-reactor OEM, the frontier of quantum.
3. **Counterparty need.** Intel was distressed; MP needed scale-up capital; LAC needed loan relief; USAR and the quantum names needed funding; Nvidia needed licenses; Nippon needed approval. Health without need = no deal (see the Micron/TSMC exemption, §4.2).
4. **Passive paper, loud politics.** Non-voting common, preferred + warrants, penny warrants, profit shares, golden shares — engineered for "taxpayer upside" headlines without formal control (control arrives informally anyway; see §8).
5. **Alignment as admission ticket.** Lip-Bu Tan went from "must resign immediately" to deal partner in two weeks — the stake *was* the reconciliation. Jensen Huang flew to Beijing "because President Trump asked me to come" ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/14/nvidias-jensen-huang-on-china-trip-trump.html)). Altman has courted the White House for over a year. Conversely, IonQ — with a short-seller cloud and an awkward Cerberus/Feinberg conflict — was left out of the quantum-9 ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/us-dept-of-commerce-awards-nine-quantum-computing-companies-2bn-in-exchange-for-non-controlling-equity-stakes/)).

### 2.3 Three event lanes (this drives everything downstream)

- **Lane A — Conversion/rescue equity.** Existing federal money is converted into stock in a chokepoint company that needs capital. *Where the biggest stock moves live* (MP +50%, TMQ +211%, LAC ~2x). Candidate pool: minerals, nuclear fuel, distressed semis, small defense-tech.
- **Lane B — Permission pricing.** Licenses, permits, and approvals are priced as revenue shares, golden shares, or warrants. Nvidia/AMD (15%→25% China revenue), US Steel, Trilogy, prospectively TMC's NOAA permit and semicap/EDA China licenses. Stock impact is *positive but muted* — the instrument taxes upside the market wasn't pricing anyway.
- **Lane C — Donation-for-relief.** The 2026 innovation: healthy AI megacaps "donate" ~5% into a public vehicle in exchange for a stable regulatory perimeter (model-access framework, copyright, preemption, antitrust posture). Proposed, not agreed; OpenAI is the icebreaker. If it lands, it maps onto GOOGL and META directly.

## 3. The screening framework

Nine factors, weighted by how consistently each shows up in the completed deals, scored 0–10 per name, composite = Σ(score×weight)/10 + penalty, on a 0–100 scale. (Full rubrics on the "Factor Definitions" tab.)

| Factor | Weight | What it captures | Anchor evidence |
|--------|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| Federal leverage hooks | 20 | Convertible grants/loans/contracts/licenses/permits already in play | Every single deal |
| Strategic chokepoint | 18 | Sole/scarce domestic source of an AI-stack or defense input | MP, INTC, LEU-pattern, Westinghouse |
| Capital need / wants something | 14 | Distress, capex hunger, or a needed permission slip | INTC, LAC, MP, USAR, NVDA |
| Mechanism feasibility | 10 | US domicile/control, digestible size, an existing deal lane | TSM/ARM haircuts; Lane C partially rescues megacaps |
| Political alignment | 10 | Courtship, pledges, rehabilitation arcs | Tan, Huang, Altman; Musk counterexample |
| National-security narrative | 10 | How cleanly the stake sells as security policy | All |
| China/adversary leverage | 8 | Export-control or supply-dependence economics | NVDA/AMD, antimony, rare earths |
| Domestic buildout | 6 | US capex the administration can attach to | INTC fabs, MP 10X, Piketon |
| Signal flow | 4 | Already floated publicly | LMT (Lutnick), CRML, GOOGL/META |
| **Penalty** | 0 to −10 | Exemption statements, short clouds, adversarial posture | MU/TSM/TXN; IONQ; QUBT |

**Why "hooks" outweighs "chokepoint":** Micron and TSMC are massive chokepoints and got *explicit* no-equity statements — because their grants were finalized and their US capex was already maximal. No open hook, no deal, regardless of strategic weight. The hook is the trigger; the chokepoint is the ammunition.

## 4. Pressure test: back-cast and falsification

### 4.1 Would the framework have caught the actual deals ex-ante?

Scoring each recipient as of the day before its announcement (same rubric, information then available):

| Deal | Ex-ante score | Would it have ranked? |
|------|---------------|----------------------|
| Intel (Aug 2025) | 88.6 | #1 in semis by a wide margin — unpaid CHIPS grants + distress + chokepoint was unique |
| MP Materials (Jul 2025) | 87.8 | #1 in materials — sole US RE mine + DPA money already flowing |
| USA Rare Earth (Jan 2026) | 81.2 | #2 in rare earths behind MP — the "second platform" call was makeable |
| GlobalFoundries (May 2026) | 75.2 | Top-3 US-domiciled foundry exposure; trusted-foundry hooks flagged |
| Lithium Americas (Oct 2025) | 73.4 | #1 in battery materials — the distressed $2.26B DOE loan was public for months |
| Trilogy (Oct 2025) | 73.0 | High — permit-dependency + Alaska politics visible |
| L3Harris unit (Dec 2025) | 67.8 | Caught by the unit-level lane only — this deal *created* that lane; the framework now encodes it |
| Quantum-9 smalls (May 2026) | 57–65 | Middling individually — the sweep was sector-wide policy, not name-picking; the framework treats "sector sweep" risk via the signal factor |

The framework's misses are honest ones: it would not have predicted *which week*, and it under-scored the quantum smalls individually because the government chose a *sector*, not companies (all nine got near-identical terms). Lesson encoded: when officials signal a sector sweep, buy the basket, not the best name.

### 4.2 Falsification cases — pattern-matches that did NOT get deals

- **Micron / TSMC / Texas Instruments:** enormous chokepoints, enormous CHIPS awards — and an explicit Commerce statement that equity will not be sought from recipients that raised US investment ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/white-house-wont-ask-for-ownership-stake-in-tsmc-or-micron-in-exchange-for-chips-act-funds-companies-already-investing-more-in-the-us-expected-to-be-exempt)). Confirms: *need + open hook* is the trigger, not strategic importance. Handled via penalty.
- **IonQ:** the largest pure-play quantum name was excluded from the quantum-9 amid a short-seller report and a DoW-official conflict-of-interest optic. Confirms: reputational cloud is disqualifying even when the sector is being swept. Handled via penalty.
- **Palantir:** maximal alignment, maximal narrative — no stake, because there is nothing to convert and no need to price. The relationship monetizes as contracts. Confirms the hooks factor's primacy.
- **Tesla/xAI-adjacent:** adversarial CEO posture coincides with total absence from every deal and dinner. Alignment is a real variable, not color.
- **Power utilities (Vistra, Constellation, Talen):** despite AI-power narrative dominance, the state transacts with them as *customer and permitter*, not owner — no conversion hook exists on a regulated balance sheet. The screen correctly scores the whole group in the 30s–40s.

## 6. Deep dives

### 6.1 PPTA — Perpetua Resources (85.0) — *the purest MP clone left*
**Thesis.** The only permitted US antimony source (Stibnite, Idaho) in a world where China has banned antimony exports; already DPA Title III-funded; EXIM issued a **$1.8B LOI**; in construction with a gold by-product that de-risks the economics. Every element of the MP pattern — chokepoint, existing federal money, capital need, security narrative — is present simultaneously, which is exactly the configuration that preceded the MP, LAC, and USAR deals.
**Mechanism path.** EXIM/DPA financing close restructured to include shares or warrants (the USAR January template applied to antimony); alternatively a DoD offtake with price floor.
**Catalysts.** Project financing close; Project Vault stockpile purchases (antimony is on every list); any DoW munitions-supply announcement (antimony = ammunition primers, penetrators).
**Kills it.** Financing closes clean without government paper; gold-driven private capital crowds the state out (the anti-thesis: gold at highs means they may not *need* Washington).
**Positioning notes.** ~$3B cap, liquid enough for size in shares; long-dated calls exist but spreads are wide — the LAC lesson says the move starts on the *leak*, so resting equity + patience beats paying theta.

### 6.2 LEU — Centrus Energy (84.2) — *the Intel conversion logic on live federal money*
**Thesis.** The only US-owned uranium enrichment path (born of the USEC privatization — the government has owned this asset before), sole HALEU producer, with a **$900M DOE task order (Jan 5, 2026)** inside an up-to-$3.4B enrichment program that *requires* matching private capital for the Piketon buildout ([ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-01-06/article-7652/doe-awards-27b-for-haleu-and-leu-enrichment/)). Russia-dependence narrative does the work China does elsewhere. Large federal money + matching-capital need + absolute chokepoint = the exact Intel configuration at 1% of the size.
**Mechanism path.** Equity/warrants attached to the task-order buildout or a follow-on tranche; DOE has every incentive to take upside on capacity it is effectively underwriting.
**Catalysts.** Piketon expansion FID and financing mix announcements; further task-order exercises; any SMR fuel-supply crunch headlines (Oklo/TerraPower fuel needs all route through HALEU).
**Kills it.** DOE keeps the relationship purely contractual (its historical preference); convertible-note issuance covers the match without state paper.
**Positioning notes.** ~$6B cap, good options market, high beta to nuclear sentiment. Note LEU already trades with a policy premium — the incremental event is partially priced; expression favors calls on definitive-agreement language rather than chasing task-order headlines.

### 6.3 UUUU — Energy Fuels (79.8) — *two doctrines, one asset*
**Thesis.** White Mesa is the only conventional uranium mill in the US *and* an operating rare-earth separation site — the only public company sitting on both the nuclear-fuel and rare-earth doctrines at once. A Trump official has said on record that [more minerals-company stakes are coming](https://www.mining.com/web/us-plans-more-stakes-in-minerals-companies-trump-official-says/), and the $12B Project Vault stockpile ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/trump-stockpile-critical-minerals-reserve-project-vault.html)) needs exactly what White Mesa produces.
**Mechanism path.** MP-style: equity/warrants + price floor + stockpile offtake around heavy-RE separation expansion.
**Catalysts.** Project Vault procurement details; heavy-RE (Dy/Tb) expansion financing; uranium reserve purchases.
**Kills it.** MP and USAR absorb all rare-earth policy bandwidth; stockpile buys arrive as plain purchase orders.
**Positioning notes.** ~$5B, liquid, optionable. The cleanest "next MP" basket is UUUU + PPTA + NB; equal-weighting the basket beats concentrating, because §4.1 shows the government sometimes sweeps sectors.

### 6.4 NB — NioCorp (73.8) — *the USAR template, queued*
Elk Creek (Nebraska): niobium/scandium/titanium, zero current domestic production of any of them; EXIM engagement (~$780M debt LOI) is the pre-positioned hook; needs the full construction stack. Microcap (~$1B) with TMQ-shaped announcement asymmetry. Kills it: EXIM passes, or dilution arrives privately first. Expression: equity only — options are untradeable; size for a binary.

### 6.5 OKLO — Oklo (72.8) — *maximum alignment meets capital intensity*
The administration's favorite nuclear startup: DOE reactor-pilot selections, INL fuel award, Altman lineage, deep MAGA-tech cap table, and the Westinghouse deal proves the government will take economics in nuclear build-out. Pre-revenue with multi-billion capex ahead = need. Mechanism: warrants attached to a DOE/DoW power-purchase + fuel package for Aurora deployments. Kills it: private capital keeps funding it (it has been able to raise at will); no conversion hook exists yet — this is the one top-10 name where the government would have to *create* the hook. Watch: Aurora INL first power; DoD microreactor awards; HALEU allocations.

### 6.6 CRML — Critical Metals Corp (72.0) — *stake talks already reported*
Tanbreez (Greenland) heavy REE; EXIM $120M LOI; **prospective US equity stake already reported** — highest signal score in the pool. Complications: Greenland politics, and the Jan 2026 Saudi processing JV cuts both ways (dilutes the "American champion" story). Microcap; leak-driven; equity only. This is the highest-risk/highest-asymmetry name in the top 10.

### 6.7 BWXT — BWX Technologies (71.0) — *the L3Harris template's next customer*
Sole-source naval nuclear components, TRISO/HALEU fuel work, national-lab operations — hooks everywhere, but zero distress, which historically meant "contracts only." The L3Harris rocket-motor structure changed that: unit-level equity in a strategic division of a *healthy* prime is now a live lane. Mechanism: government economics in a carved-out commercial-fuel or microreactor unit. Watch: any BWXT commercial-HALEU unit reorganization; Aerojet IPO pricing (H2 2026) — if it prices well, this lane opens wide.

### 6.8 TMC — TMC the metals co. (71.0) — *the next permit priced as equity*
NOAA ruled the consolidated deep-sea application in full compliance (Mar 2026), permit decision expected early 2027, resource of 619Mt nodules ([company](https://investors.metals.co/news-releases/news-release-details/tmc-usa-files-first-consolidated-deep-seabed-mining-application), [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/tmc-nears-deep-sea-mining-permit-targets-2027-start/)). Trilogy proved permits get priced as equity; the seabed EO created this lane deliberately. Mechanism: equity/royalty + stockpile offtake attached to the recovery permit. Kills it: permit slips past the administration's attention window; ISA/diplomatic blowback. Note +450% in 2025 — the policy premium is partially in; the *stake* event is not.

### 6.9 LMT — Lockheed Martin (69.6) — *the only name an official has said out loud*
Lutnick, on record: the administration is "thinking about" defense-contractor stakes, citing Lockheed's 97% government revenue ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/business/5470771-trump-administration-defense-contractors/)). Total revenue dependence = total leverage, but no crisis to price against, so expect *structure* — golden-share-style rights, or equity in a munitions/space unit — rather than an Intel-scale purchase. At $110B, a 5% stake is fiscally trivial ($5.5B) and politically enormous. Watch: F-35 negotiation flashpoints, munitions-surge supplemental language, any "taxpayer upside" phrasing near a Lockheed contract award. Expression: cheap long-dated upside via options makes sense here precisely because the event is low-probability/high-surprise.

### 6.10 HII — Huntington Ingalls (69.0) — *where defense "need" actually lives*
Shipbuilding is the loudest budget priority ($65.8B in the FY27 request; 30-year plan) and HII is the sole-ish builder of carriers/amphibs with real workforce/capex strain — the only prime with a genuine *need* score. Mechanism: shipyard-modernization package with warrants, sold as reindustrialization. Kills it: money keeps flowing as plain appropriations (the historical norm).

### 6.11 MU — Micron (66.6) — *the great disqualified name*
Every fundamental says yes (only US memory/HBM, $200B+ US buildout, China leverage); the policy record says no — Commerce explicitly exempted investing-in-America recipients from equity. MU enters the portfolio only if it ever *needs* something new from Washington (a Secure-Enclave-scale program, HBM export-control relief). Treat as a dormant candidate; the trigger to re-rate is any MU request for federal money/permission, at which point it jumps ~15 points.

### 6.12 GOOGL — Alphabet (62.6) and the Lane C complex
Named (with Meta and Anthropic) in the reported 5%-each Public Wealth Fund concept. The trade here is *not* "government buys Alphabet" — it is "Alphabet buys a stable regulatory perimeter with treasury shares." Watch three things: (1) the **Aug 1, 2026** model-access framework deliverable — the first institutional plumbing between labs and the security state; (2) whether OpenAI's donation actually papers (the icebreaker); (3) any linkage language between DOJ-remedy matters and "national AI champions." For META, same lane, higher alignment, less antitrust urgency. Impact math: a 5% donation is ~2 quarters of buyback — dilution noise, sentiment event; the signal value (government as aligned shareholder vs. adversarial regulator) is worth more than the paper. Probability: meaningful but below the Lane A names; nothing about Lane C is agreed.

### 6.13 The NVDA/AMD lane (83.0 / 73.2) — *recurring, tradeable, mispriced as one-offs*
Nvidia's China licenses have now been priced twice (15% H20 → reported 25% H200), and Huang's Beijing-delegation seat confirms the relationship is transactional and ongoing. Each licensing cycle is a tradeable event with a known template: license grant = revenue the market had zeroed out, minus the government's cut = net positive surprise. The unpriced tail: this template *spreading* — semicap China tools (LRCX/AMAT/KLAC all score 53–54 on exactly this lane) and EDA (SNPS/CDNS) are one export-control memo away from the same economics. An actual NVDA *equity* event would require the Lane C vehicle; at $4.7T, only a donation-model works.

## 7. Trading playbook

### 7.1 What the reaction study says

| Pattern | Evidence | Implication |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Small caps re-rate violently on day 1 | TMQ +211%, MP +50%, LAC ~2x on leak | The move is a *gap*, not a drift — you must be positioned before the tape |
| The leak precedes the announcement by days-to-weeks | LAC: Reuters Sep 24 → deal Oct 7; Intel: Bloomberg leaks preceded by a week+ | First credible wire report ≈ 50–100% of the small-cap move; treat leaks as the event |
| Large caps move modestly on the stake, then re-rate on the *meaning* | INTC +7% day 1, ~5x over 10 months | The stake is a floor + a signal that the state is now invested in the equity going up; post-announcement entry still worked in the largest case |
| Sector sweeps lift excluded names too | IONQ +12%, QUBT +19%, ARQQ +25% on a package they weren't in | Sympathy beta is real; baskets outperform single-name precision when a sector sweep is signaled |
| Permission-pricing events are muted | NVDA/AMD rev-share | Don't pay event premium for Lane B names; own them for fundamentals with the license cycle as free optionality |

### 7.2 Expression rules derived from the above

1. **Lane A small caps (PPTA, NB, CRML, TMC, UAMY, RCAT):** resting equity positions, sized for binaries. Options where they exist are structurally mispriced *after* leaks, not before; the pre-leak asymmetry is in the shares. Basket logic over conviction logic (§4.1 sweep lesson).
2. **Lane A mid caps (LEU, UUUU, OKLO, BWXT, HII):** equity core + opportunistic long-dated calls when policy headlines are quiet (IV resets). These names carry partial policy premium already — the incremental event is definitive-agreement language, not chatter.
3. **Lane B (NVDA, AMD, and prospectively LRCX/AMAT/KLAC/SNPS/CDNS):** no event premium warranted; the license cycle adds asymmetric *positive* surprises to names you'd own anyway. Watch Commerce export-license dockets.
4. **Lane C (GOOGL, META):** the stake, if it comes, is a sentiment/multiple event, not a cash event. The cheap expression is owning the names into the **Aug 1 framework** and any OpenAI-donation close, against which the market has priced ~nothing.
5. **Already-staked second tranches (INTC warrant/foundry-spin trigger, MP 10X, USAR definitive docs, LHX Aerojet IPO H2-26, quantum LOI→definitive closes):** each has a dated, watchable catalyst — these are the highest-visibility events in the whole space and the market keeps treating them as one-offs.
6. **What not to do:** don't chase day-1 gaps in microcaps (TMQ round-tripped much of its spike); don't own the power/utility complex for stake risk (wrong instrument — they get contracts); don't assume announcement = top (Intel is the counterexample that keeps giving).

## 8. Pressure test / bear case — what breaks the thesis

- **Legal/appropriations challenge.** House Natural Resources Democrats have already [demanded oversight of the mineral equity deals](https://democrats-naturalresources.house.gov/imo/media/doc/2026-02-02_moc_to_defense_commerce_energy_interior_re_mineral_equity_deals_oversight.pdf); the CHIPS-conversion authority has never been litigated. A successful challenge freezes Lane A. *Mitigant:* deals to date were structured around existing statutory hooks precisely to survive this; warrants/LOIs would survive a freeze on new common purchases.
- **Political reversal, Nov 2026.** A Congress hostile to the program can choke new appropriations-backed deals — but cannot easily unwind executed stakes, and the 2028 tail risk runs the *other* way (a successor selling stakes = overhang on staked names; flag on INTC especially, where the government's $40B+ unrealized gain is itself a future supply event).
- **Crowding.** This theme is no longer secret — TMC +450% in 2025, LEU's policy premium, quantum sympathy moves. The screen's edge decays fastest in names where "signal" is the top factor (CRML). *Mitigant:* the market still prices these as one-off events rather than a programmatic pipeline; the pipeline view (sector sweeps, second tranches, lane-spreading to semicap/EDA) is where consensus remains thin.
- **The government's presence distorts the object it touches.** Nvidia's 25% China cut compresses margins; a Public Wealth Fund stake invites permanent political interference in capital allocation (buybacks, layoffs, siting). Long-horizon holders of staked names are underwriting an unpriced governance regime. For the front-running strategy this is *helpful* (events keep coming); for the holding strategy it is the core risk.
- **Honest disconfirmers to track:** Commerce has now *twice* narrowed the program's scope with exemption statements (TSMC/Micron; "not looking to take equity from companies already investing"). If that language broadens — e.g., "equity only for pre-revenue frontier tech" — the LMT/HII/BWXT defense lane dies and the screen's weights need rebalancing toward small caps only.
